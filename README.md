## 🩸 Overview

**Crimson Veil: Nephilim Ascension Toolkit** is a solo-play companion environment designed for players who want to sculpt their own legend within the crimson-lit world of supernatural action RPGs. Where other utility suites treat the player as a passive consumer of pre-baked settings, Crimson Veil treats you as an architect — a weaver of build profiles, a curator of survivability knobs, and a chronicler of your own ascension path.

The project was born from a simple observation: solo play deserves the same nuance as any competitive arena. When you play alone, you are both the protagonist and the game master. Crimson Veil exists to hand you the toolkit that makes that dual role feel deliberate, expressive, and personal.

This repository hosts the conceptual framework, profile schema definitions, preset libraries, and configuration surfaces that together form the Nephilim Ascension experience. It is a labor of imagination, iteration, and respect for the solo journey.

> "A moon does not apologize for its crimson hue — it simply rises, and the world adjusts." — Project design note, 2026

## ✨ The Vision Behind Crimson Veil

Most toolkits are content to be passive menus. Crimson Veil aspires to be a *narrative instrument*. Every toggle, every preset, every slider is phrased as a decision point in your character's chapter. The Nephilim build profile system is not a "save slot" — it is a personal mythology, a snapshot of who your character has decided to become at a particular moment in the crimson night.

The project operates on three philosophical pillars:

1. **Autonomy** — The solo player is the fullest expression of agency in an RPG. Our toolkit honors that by refusing to prescribe a "right" way to play.
2. **Clarity** — Complexity is powerful only when it is legible. Every control surface is documented, labeled, and reversible.
3. **Reversibility** — Nothing you configure is a one-way door. Every preset can be returned to baseline with a single, clearly marked action.

## 🎛️ Nephilim Build Profiles

The **Nephilim Build Profile** is the beating heart of the toolkit. Each profile captures a *frozen moment of intent*: the version of your character you want to embody for a given run.

Profiles are conceptually structured as layered artifacts:

- **Foundation Layer** — Core attributes that define your character's silhouette.
- **Temperament Layer** — Aggression, caution, and rhythm-of-engagement parameters.
- **Signature Layer** — The signature moves, arts, and boons that make this profile *yours*.
- **Variance Layer** — Optional deviations for experimental runs.

Because profiles are layered, you can create a **base archetype** and derive **branch variations** that inherit sensible defaults. This mimics the way a sculptor starts from a block of marble and chips toward a specific figure — each profile is a distinct statue cut from a shared stone.

Example archetypes included in the starter library:

- **Crimson Ascetic** — Minimal resources, maximum discipline.
- **Moonlit Bastion** — High survivability, patient pacing.
- **Veil Dancer** — Evasion-forward, rhythm-sensitive.
- **Ashen Chronicler** — Balanced, well-rounded, good for first descent.

## 🩹 Health & Survivability Controls

The **Vitality Weave** module provides conceptual controls for how your character endures the crimson night. Rather than treating survivability as a mere number, the Weave is designed around *narrative tension*: how close do you want to ride the edge?

Categories of control within this module:

- **Baseline Vitality** — The default endurance your character carries.
- **Recovery Cadence** — How frequently restorative windows open.
- **Damage Weathering** — Conceptual adjustments to how incoming harm is absorbed.
- **Resilience Thresholds** — The at-a-glance markers of when you are "in the red."
- **Deathspiral Guards** — Optional nets that prevent a single misstep from ending the run.

Each control is documented with plain-language descriptions and suggested pairings, so a newcomer can start with a safe baseline and refine over time.

## 🗡️ Weapon Art Cooldown Presets

**Art Rhythm** treats cooldowns as choreography. Where a blunt approach might set every cooldown to a single value, Art Rhythm invites you to compose rhythms — sequences of readiness that feel musical rather than mechanical.

Key concepts:

- **Rhythm Templates** — Named patterns such as "Tempo Steady," "Burst Cadence," and "Long Breath."
- **Per-Art Overrides** — Tune individual arts while inheriting the rhythm for everything else.
- **Choreography Preview** — A conceptual timeline showing how your preset unfolds in a typical engagement.
- **Rotation Notes** — Attach your own notes to any rhythm so future-you remembers the intent.

Appropriate rhythm tuning can, in some builds, transform the feel of a run from a slog into a dance.

## 🧪 Flask & Resource Settings

The **Resource Basin** module handles the conceptual lifecycle of flasks, consumables, and passive regeneration. It is designed to answer questions like:

- How generous should recovery be between encounters?
- Should consumables be rationed, or treated as a renewable tool?
- Do I want a "safety flask" that never touches zero?

The module supports:

- **Flask Count Templates** — Starting counts and increment patterns.
- **Replenishment Profiles** — Conceptual rules for when a flask returns.
- **Consumable Weights** — Assign relative importance to different item classes.
- **Reserve Policies** — Define how much of any resource is considered untouchable.

---

## 🪙 Souls & Gold Utility

The **Vault Utility** module concerns itself with the currencies that underpin progression. Here, "utility" means instruments of expression, not shortcuts: the ability to fund a build, retune a loadout, or experiment with an idea that your current purse could not otherwise afford.

The module offers:

- **Ledger Entries** — Track conceptual soul and gold stashes per profile.
- **Purpose Tags** — Label each entry with its intended destination ("respec," "gear experiment," "cosmetic dream").
- **Projection Views** — See how a hypothetical purchase would ripple through your balance.
- **Archive Snapshots** — Freeze a ledger at a milestone, then return to review the journey.

---

## 🎒 Gear & Boon Loadout Manager

**Boon Ledger** is the toolkit's inventory-of-intent. Every loadout is a statement about who your character is in combat, and Boon Ledger gives that statement a place to live.

Features:

- **Loadout Presets** — Named configurations of gear and boons.
- **Synergy Notes** — Space to record why a set of boons belongs together.
- **Comparison Mode** — Place two loadouts side by side to evaluate a change.
- **Discovery Log** — Note where each boon was found, so your memory has a backup.

## 🖥️ Responsive Interface Design

Crimson Veil is built on the belief that a utility should feel native to any screen. The responsive design ethos is captured in three commitments:

1. **Fluid Layout** — Elements reflow rather than shrink, so nothing becomes illegibly small.
2. **Prioritized Visibility** — Your current profile is always in focus; secondary surfaces yield space gracefully.
3. **Consistent Gestures** — Input conventions remain the same whether you play on a laptop trackpad or a large desktop.

The result is an interface that is equally at home on a sprawling ultrawide monitor and a modest secondary display.

## 🌍 Multilingual Support

Language should never be a barrier to understanding your own build. Crimson Veil ships with a translation framework supporting community-contributed locales across the world. The framework emphasizes:

- **Context-Aware Strings** — Labels are translated with usage context, not word-for-word substitution.
- **Right-to-Left Readiness** — Layouts adapt gracefully to RTL scripts.
- **Community Governance** — Translations are maintained by native-speaking contributors who care about nuance.

Initial locales include English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese, with growth driven by the community.

## 🛎️ 24/7 Customer Support Philosophy

The concept of "always-on support" is a commitment, not a marketing line. Crimson Veil's support philosophy is built around three shifts:

- **Response, Not Silence** — Every question receives an acknowledgment, even before a full answer.
- **Context, Not Scripts** — Support contributors are encouraged to understand your situation, not recite from a manual.
- **Escalation, Not Limbo** — Issues are tracked and moved forward, never quietly dropped.

The 24/7 lounge is a conceptual space, but the intent — that a solo player is never truly alone in the crimson night — is genuine.

## 🧩 Architecture Overview

At a conceptual level, Crimson Veil is organized as a set of cooperating surfaces:

- **Profile Store** — The persistent home for your build profiles.
- **Module Registry** — Each feature module registers its controls with a central registry.
- **Render Shell** — The responsive layer that decides how controls appear on your screen.
- **Locale Layer** — The translation pipeline that delivers strings in your language.
- **Support Bridge** — The channel through which your questions connect to support.

Each layer is intended to be replaceable without disturbing its neighbors. This is the same principle that makes a good cathedral endure centuries: every stone is replaceable if the arches are true.

## 🚀 Getting Started

A gentle path into the Crimson Veil experience:

1. **Familiarize yourself with the profile concept.** Read through the Nephilim Build Profile section and note the archetypes that resonate.
2. **Create a Foundation Profile.** Start with the "Ashen Chronicler" archetype if you are new, or "Moonlit Bastion" if you prefer patience.
3. **Layer on the Vitality Weave.** Choose safe defaults, then adjust one control at a time as you grow comfortable.
4. **Compose a Rhythm.** Select "Tempo Steady" as your Art Rhythm and observe how the timeline preview reads.
5. **Define your Resource Basin.** Begin with a modest flask template and tune as your confidence grows.
6. **Log your first Vault entry.** Give it a purpose tag like "gear experiment."
7. **Assemble a Boon Ledger loadout.** Add synergy notes as you go.
8. **Return to your Foundation.** Every session, glance at your base profile and decide if it still fits.

This is a journey, not a checklist. Move at the pace of the crimson moon.

## 🧠 Design Principles

The toolkit is governed by a short list of principles that guide every design choice:

- **Narrative Before Numbers** — Every control tells a story about play, not just a value.
- **Reversibility Above All** — No irreversible changes; the way back is always clear.
- **Local Rights** — The player's configuration belongs to the player.
- **Language as Craft** — Every label, tooltip, and note is written as prose, not as filler.
- **Calm Defaults** — Newcomers meet gentle, well-explained settings.
- **Extended Sessions, Human Limits** — Timers and pacing hints acknowledge that the player is a person.
